Mohammed Ali

Founder & Head Trainer

Mohammed has over 20 years of fitness-industry experience. He began as a trainer in 2005, worked freelance from 2009–2012, and founded Body Ethics Fitness Studio in Thoraipakkam, which operated from 2012 to 2019. He launched Origins Fitness Studio in Madipakkam in June 2025.

Studio profile

Karan Matthew

Senior Personal Fitness Trainer · NCVET certified

Karan has over seven years of professional experience and an NCVET certification, bringing a structured understanding of health and wellness to his clients. A three-time District Level Men's Physique Champion, he specializes in strength and conditioning, muscle building, fat loss, functional training, and form correction. He uses goal-based programming, consistent progress tracking, and personalized workouts tailored to each individual's needs.

Studio profile

Afzal

General Trainer · 3+ years of experience

Afzal is a dedicated General Trainer at Origins Fitness Studio with over three years of professional fitness coaching experience. He guides clients toward becoming stronger and healthier through fitness, discipline, and tangible results. His expertise covers strength training, muscle building, high-intensity interval training (HIIT), and core conditioning. He specializes in fat loss, weight management, and full body transformations, and provides basic nutrition and dietary guidance alongside physical workouts. Through structured guidance and personal dedication, he helps clients work toward lasting lifestyle changes and their personal fitness goals.

Studio profile

Sanjay

General Trainer · Over 1 year of coaching experience

Sanjay is a General Trainer at Origins Fitness Studio with over a year of coaching experience. Focused on helping clients transform their lives, he specializes in weight loss, muscle building, high-intensity interval training (HIIT), and strength and core training. His approach emphasizes safety, proper warm-up and cool-down routines, and continuous progress tracking to help clients become stronger, fitter, and healthier.
